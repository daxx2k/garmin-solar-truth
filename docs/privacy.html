Solar Truth Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026. Developer: Davide La Sala.

Data processed on your watch

Solar Truth reads timestamps, solar intensity, watch battery percentage, estimated battery runtime and cable-charging status to estimate the benefit of sunlight. It stores a rolling buffer of up to 288 samples, up to 31 daily summaries and accumulated calibration totals in the app's local watch storage. Calibration totals can remain until reset or the app data is removed. Diagnostic status and text logs may also be stored on the watch.

Transmission and optional exports

The app does not require an account and does not transmit readings to the developer or to an app-operated server. It contains no advertising or analytics service. Optional diagnostic backups can write readings to text logs or FIT activity files. FIT files may be synchronized to Garmin services by the watch and Garmin software, subject to your Garmin settings and Garmin's privacy policies. If you manually share a diagnostic file with the developer, the developer receives its contents for support; please inspect it before sending.

Permissions

Background access enables periodic logging while the app is closed. FIT-related permissions support optional diagnostic exports. The app does not request location, contacts or internet communications permissions.

Control and deletion

The diagnostics panel offers a calibration reset action; this resets calibration totals, not the recorded readings or exported files. Uninstalling the app removes its application data according to Garmin device behavior. Exported text or FIT files, backups and copies already synchronized or shared may need to be deleted separately. Diagnostic files voluntarily sent for support are retained only as needed to resolve the support request.

Contact

Contact Davide La Sala using the developer contact option on the Solar Truth Connect IQ Store listing. Data you voluntarily submit to the developer is submitted to the developer, not to Garmin; Garmin is not responsible for the developer's handling of that data.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
